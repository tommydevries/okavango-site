how we work

Most consulting ends at the recommendation. We build the thing, put it in front of your customers, and get paid on what it produces, not on the hours it took.

A pilot is a real system, running in your business, tied to a baseline we agree on before it starts. You carry no build cost and no license fee. If it moves the number, we share in the result. If it does not, you owe nothing. That structure only works when we are confident in the work, which is exactly the point.
