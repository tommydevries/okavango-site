What happens in the room

Stop using AI like a search box. Have Claude do the work.

1

Learn the method.

A simple way to work with AI: what to hand off, how to brief it, and how to check what comes back.

2

Set Claude up on real work.

Chasing invoices, organizing notes, running reports. You pick the task. Trainers in the room help you get it working.

3

Leave with it handed off.

You walk out with Claude doing that task. Claude drafts and recommends. You decide.
